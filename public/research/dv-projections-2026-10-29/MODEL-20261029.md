# Current model and measurement record — October 29, 2026

The current site artifact is DV Projections v1.3. The tree/ridge recipes, 50/50 model blend, 70/30 horizon/aging blend and price settings are unchanged. Two input/workload changes are applied before the current free-agent estimates:

- Historical FanGraphs leaderboards encode innings as baseball notation: 174.2 means 174 and two outs. The unlocked runtime loader converts those actual season innings to decimal innings before building features, targets, healthy workloads and league baselines. Archived projected innings are continuous expectations and are **not** converted. The original hash-locked source and scores remain unchanged as historical evidence.
- Current percentile mappings use the same WAR/workload feature version as the projection they normalize. Starters and relievers have separate pitcher maps. Mapped reliever innings are capped at the archived reliever 80th percentile; recent healthy workload may supply a higher floor. The first-year totals average about 0.095 WAR lower for relievers, 0.025 higher for starters and 0.001 higher for position players than the previous public artifact. Hitter rate estimates are unchanged; Rafaela's displayed WAR, rates and workloads are unchanged.

`validation/independent_primary_replay.json` refits the original locked recipe and recomputes primary scores directly from raw outcomes and preseason arrays, without using the original scoring functions. All sample counts match; the largest RMSE discrepancy is less than 4e-14. `validation/current_primary_results.json` gives the current rates with actual innings normalized and paired player-clustered uncertainty. Current FIP RMSE is .7282, versus .7629 for ZiPS and .7312 for Steamer; the main comparison remains favorable against ZiPS and inconclusive against Steamer.

`FULL-SEASON-VALIDATION.md` defines the additional all-player season-WAR endpoint before it was scored. `validation/full_season_war_results.json` includes established players with zero target-year MLB workload and reports expected versus valuation workloads separately. Expected-workload RMSE is 1.1355 (hitters) and .8027 (pitchers), versus ZiPS 1.3999/.8767 and Steamer 1.1574/.8041. The current role-based full-season valuation policy gives 1.2325/.8696 and is positively biased for realized season WAR. This is why valuation workload is not described as an injury-adjusted expectation.

The normalized horizon replay has 16/17 lower-error direct horizon results and 17/17 lower-error 70/30 blends against the historical aging baseline. These are point-estimate counts from reused seasons. They do not represent 17 independent samples, and they do not validate survival, smoothing or prices on their own.

The original first-run generator composite hash in `site/replay_metadata.json` refers to the three preserved files under `provenance/`. The earlier complete release is preserved as `provenance/pr3-release-20261029.tar.gz`. Current source hashes and input hashes accompany the additional validation; `site/current_build_verification.json` records the artifact change and the unchanged Rafaela path.

The full-season endpoint and normalized model checks are retrospective checks on previously examined seasons, not a fresh holdout. No parameter was optimized against 2027 contracts. A prospective test requires saving the chosen forecast and dated competitors before their target season.
