# DV Projections v1.4 — October 30, 2026

One change from v1.3: in the five-year WAR-rate blend (70% horizon model, 30% one-year rate extended by an aging rule), the aging rule is now the site's fixed age policy (`lib/aging-curve.mjs` `empiricalRateChange`, exported to `site/fixed_age_policy.json`; position players by catcher, up-the-middle and corner, starters, and relievers converted from WAR/65 IP to WAR/180 IP), instead of the historical delta-method curve. Smoothing, workloads, the first projected season and everything else are unchanged.

Test (`site/exp3c_aging.py`, aggregate results `site/aging_v14_results.json`): horizon models refit under the forecast-origin rules in `AMENDMENT-TIMING.md`, from origins 2018, 2019 and 2021 (targets through 2024; specified before any result in `analysis/projection_v2_dev/PROTOCOL.md`) and 2022-24 (targets through 2026, reused seasons). Error is PA/IP-weighted RMSE of WAR per 600 PA or 180 IP for targets with 200+ PA or 40+ IP, after the site's smoothing; intervals resample players.

| Sample | Hitters | Pitchers |
|---|---|---|
| Origins 2018, 2019, 2021 | -4.0% [-5.2, -2.7] | -0.6% [-0.8, -0.3] |
| Origins 2022-24 | -0.3% [-0.8, +0.1] | -0.3% [-0.6, -0.1] |

The gain grows with horizon (hitters -0.9% at two years to -6.7% at five in the first sample). In the first sample some long horizons had no observable training outcomes, so the aging rule supplied the whole forecast there; that inflates the hitter gain in that sample. A first version of this test, run on the pre-correction horizon code, is superseded.

Rebuild: `site_multiyear_v14.py --input dvp_2027_2031_v3.json --policy site/fixed_age_policy.json --output dvp_multiyear_v14.json`, then `build_dv_site.py` as in the README; the published JSON then has its meta label set to v1.4. The v1.3 artifact was first rebuilt from the same inputs and matched the published file byte for byte.

Effect: 2027 unchanged; later seasons +0.03 (2028) to +0.09 (2031) WAR on average; total DV surplus +14% (from 46% to 39% below ZiPS-based totals). The October 29 free-agent estimates were recorded with v1.3 and are not changed.
