# Market contract model — fixed November 6, 2026 before candidate scoring

Purpose: separate expected market contracts from current DV production values, and remove projected-WAR thresholds from current contract quotes. This is a market model, not a change to player performance projections.

Use the saved FanGraphs contract tracker, 2020–26. Keep Free Agent records marked GUARANTEED with at least $2M nominal AAV. Remove apparent accepted QOs (one year, total exactly equal to the QO). Deduplicate player/class by largest guaranteed total to avoid later prorated re-signings. Retain counts/exclusions. Require at least 100 PA or 20 innings in the previous three seasons. No 2027 contracts or public 2027 forecasts are training inputs. The historical file does not independently certify signing dates/Opening Day availability or all opt-out/deferral economics: describe tests as retrospective rolling class checks, not new untouched, timing-certified holdouts.

Inputs use only three seasons through class year minus one, numeric FG identities, normalized historical innings, and age on the prior season leaderboard plus one. Derive pitcher role from prior usage; do not use post-signing position/team. Hitters include their pre-origin positional value rather than a future position label. Never use target-season performance, signing team, public contract predictions or contract outcomes in features.

Fixed features: age and age squared; role indicators and age interactions; previous-season and 5/4/3 exposure-weighted WAR rates, total WAR and workload; prior-three-year history/exposure, two-season healthy workload, recent workload change, and consecutive strong seasons. Hitter features: batting/fielding/positional/baserunning runs per 600, wRC+, ISO, BB/K rates, HR/RBI/SB counts. Pitcher features: ERA/FIP, K/BB rates, fastball velocity, games/starts, saves/holds and wins. Cap no individual player's forecast by name. Structural missing features are zero; measurements missing within applicable roles are imputed from each training split only.

One candidate: standardized ridge regression with fixed alpha=10, separately predicting log guaranteed total and log years. Predict market dollars in 2026 equivalents (fixed 3% annual adjustment); reverse the adjustment for the forecast class. Expected duration is continuous, clamped to 1–12. Display nearest whole year as an approximate term; the predicted total is independent of rounding the duration, so a 0.01 WAR change cannot create a multi-year jump in dollar value. AAV is total divided by the displayed term. Current-model production value is computed over that same quoted term. Low-history/unmodeled international cases retain explicit separate handling.

Train 2020–22 and score 2023; expand through each preceding class to score 2024, 2025, 2026. Compare on identical rows against: prior-class role medians; and the unchanged legacy pricing/term formula anchored to the same pre-origin 5/4/3 regressed WAR proxy plus its healthy workload (not a claimed historical v1.5 replay). Report all four years, roles, term/dollar/AAV errors, player-cluster bootstrap intervals and leave-one-class-out summaries. Primary: mean absolute log total error; secondary annual salary log error and term MAE. Also report a common >=$10M total group but do not select the model from it.

For deployment require a material (at least 5%) reduction in broad total-log error versus both references, improvement in at least three of four class years, no material deterioration in the 2026 class, and reasonably compatible term/AAV results. These guides do not make the historical data untouched. If unsupported, retain the candidate as research and deploy only the current-input/presentation and continuous-pricing fixes with accurately limited claims.

Validation: independent arithmetic, unique identity/class joins, all feature origins precede targets, mutation of future performance/contract attributes leaves features unchanged, train-only imputation/scaling, exact coefficient replay in JavaScript, and current forecast continuity. Preserve the frozen October 29 release unchanged. Package aggregate metrics, coefficients and code; do not redistribute raw FanGraphs leaderboards or tracker records.
