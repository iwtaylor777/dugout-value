# Revision R1 of the DV free-agent estimates — October 29, 2026

Revision R1 makes two editorial changes to the October 29 snapshot. It was recorded before any contract in this class was observed; the time is in `LOCK-DV-20261029-R1.json`.

1. **Tarik Skubal's term is set by hand to 10 years.** The model's term rule stops at nine years, because his 2036 forecast (0.89 WAR) falls below one win. That gives nine years and $367.0M. The top of the market has given aces longer deals than the rule allows, so the scored term is 10 years. The tenth season is priced from the market values recorded in the original snapshot with the same 10% discounting. The total, $385.8M, and the AAV, $38.6M, still come from the model. The row keeps the model's own numbers in `manual_override`.
2. **Jake Bauers is added as a note, not a priced row.** I expect Milwaukee to make him a qualifying offer and him to accept it, so he isn't expected to sign a market contract. If he declines it and signs elsewhere, his contract will be reported against that expectation rather than scored against a price.

Every other row, flag, exclusion and setting is unchanged. The original snapshot is in `archive/2026-10-29-dv-original/` and still matches `LOCK-DV-20261029.json`, whose conventions in `DV-20261029.md` still apply. `code/scripts/revise-dv-free-agents.mjs` checks the original against its lock, applies these two changes and writes the root table and the R1 lock.
