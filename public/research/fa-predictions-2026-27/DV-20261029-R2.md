# Revision R2 of the DV free-agent estimates — October 29, 2026

Revision R2 corrects ages. The site database built each player's 2027 age by adding a year to his current age from RosterResource. A season age is the age on June 30, so players with birthdays between July and the October refresh came out a year too old: 511 of the 1,447 major leaguers in the database. The database now takes season ages from FanGraphs' leaderboards, and the refresh script does the same.

In this table, 16 listed players and 11 excluded or next-in-line players were a year too old. `code/scripts/revise-dv-free-agents-r2.mjs` reads revision R1 (checked against `LOCK-DV-20261029-R1.json` and copied to `archive/2026-10-29-dv-r1/`), sets `age_2027` from the corrected database, and reprices every row with the unchanged pricing code. It stops if any listed price or term moves, and none did: the listed terms are set by projected WAR, not by the age limits. One reference price for an excluded player changed. Drew Rasmussen, expected to have his club option exercised, goes from 5 years and $114.7M to 6 years and $125.6M. Every change is listed in `revision.age_changes`.

R1's changes stand: Skubal's 10-year term set by hand and the note on Jake Bauers. The time is in `LOCK-DV-20261029-R2.json`.
